# Content and Writing Rules

## ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English (HARD)

Use ASD-STE100 style for English explanatory prose in user-facing and
agent-facing output. Apply its clarity rules without a strict
controlled-dictionary check.

- Use active voice, simple verb forms, and direct statements.
- Keep instructions to 20 words or fewer. Keep descriptions to 25 words or fewer.
- Put a condition before its action. Put one action in each instruction.
- Use one term for one concept throughout a response.
- Use short, concrete words. Define essential technical terms at first use.
- State facts, limits, and uncertainty directly. Do not hedge without a real reason.
- Remove filler, hype, vague praise, marketing language, and needless summaries.
- Avoid long noun clusters. Split them into clear phrases or short sentences.

Do not change verbatim code, commands, structured data, names, identifiers,
error text, citations, quotations, or supplied source material to meet these
rules. Do not apply these rules to translations, localization, language-learning
output, or requested non-English output.

**Quality test:** Can a reader who is not a native English speaker act correctly after one read? If not, rewrite.
